What the copy changes
Only live_bnb_boll_fade and live_bnb_boll_classic reach apply_news_isolation,
after the existing guards and the existing top-cap. Other purposes are unchanged.
The call does not open public_derivatives.db, cityu_institutional.db,
lseg_macro.db, or x_watch, even when those files and env paths exist.
With no crisis and no Fed hawkish shock, the original candidate objects and
size_pct are returned. Crisis drops BUY and keeps SELL (state 5, cash_halt).
Fed hawkish applies the state-4 template: size x0.55, tp x0.80, trailing off,
LIMIT_MAKER, require_perp_beta_hedge as a label only. Kelly is not applied.
aligned_rank_weight stays None. A missing, locked, timed-out, or unreadable
news database is fail-open and is written on the tick payload as news_gate.

apply_five_state_regime_router is unchanged and is still what origin/main's
engine calls. This host must not receive that engine while the three databases
exist, because that call classifies from counterparty and CityU with no switch.
The isolation function does not call it.
